About the Student ERP Portal

The Student ERP Portal is a web-based student management system designed to provide important academic information in one place. Students can log in and access their personal profile, attendance, subjects, results, timetable and university notices. The purpose of this portal is to make academic information easy to access and simple to understand.

Student ERP Features

Student Profile

View personal information, course details, skills and other student information.

Attendance

View overall attendance and subject-wise attendance percentage.

Academic Results

View marks, grades, SPI, CGPA and result status.

Timetable

View the weekly class schedule, subjects and classroom details.
