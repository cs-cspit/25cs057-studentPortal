Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in?

Enter your Student ID and password on the login page. Then click the Login button to open your Student ERP dashboard.

How do I check my attendance?

After logging in, click the Attendance option from the sidebar. You can view your overall attendance and subject-wise attendance.

How do I view my results?

Click the Results option from the sidebar. You can view your marks, grades, SPI, CGPA and result status.

How do I view my timetable?

Click the Timetable option from the sidebar to view your weekly class schedule and classroom information.
